# The pipeline

Behind every chat with Yinian is a real animation production, the same six stages a studio would go through. Yinian's AI agent works through them for you, in order, keeping a live plan in the chat so you can always see where things stand. You can step in at any stage and change anything just by saying so.

| Stage | What Yinian does | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Script** (剧本) | Grows your idea or novel into an outline and a script | Read it, ask for changes |
| **Casting** (定妆) | Designs each character and locks the look | Approve or adjust the designs |
| **Storyboard** (拆镜) | Breaks the script into shots | Skim the shot list |
| **Images** (生图) | Draws a keyframe per shot, sample first | Check the sample, re-roll any shot |
| **Video & voice** (视频) | Animates each shot, adds voiceover | Confirm the cost, then wait |
| **Final cut** (成片) | Edits everything into one film | Download the mp4, or the package |

## Script (剧本)

Everything starts with your idea. A single line is enough: Yinian expands it into an outline, then a full episode script. If you drop in a novel instead, it reads the whole thing in parts and splits it into episodes first. Ask for any change in your own words, such as a sadder ending, a shorter scene or an extra character.

## Casting (定妆)

The classic failure of AI animation is a lead who looks different in every shot. So before any shot is drawn, Yinian designs each character and locks a reference look, and every later image is generated against it. Want a different look? Say so, and shots made afterwards follow the new design.

## Storyboard (拆镜)

The script becomes a shot list: one row per shot, with framing (wide, medium, close-up…), on-screen action and timing. This is the backbone of the production; every later stage hangs off a shot here. You don't have to touch it, but you can ask Yinian to add, cut, merge or reorder shots at any time.

## Images (生图)

Yinian draws a keyframe for every shot. It starts with a sample of the first few shots so you can check the look before it does the rest. Any single shot can be redrawn on its own without disturbing its neighbours.

## Video & voice (视频)

Keyframes become moving shots with sound, plus voiceover where the scene needs it. Before rendering, Yinian tells you what it will cost and asks you to confirm; set a session budget if you'd rather it went ahead on its own. Every job shows its status, failures are explained in plain language, and a failed or timed-out job is never resubmitted behind your back, so you can't be charged twice for the same shot.

## Final cut (成片)

Yinian edits the finished shots, in order, into a single mp4 with smooth transitions, ready to download and share. If you'd like to do the edit yourself, it can export a package instead: one folder per shot plus a shot list, ready for CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or any other editor.
