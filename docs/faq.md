# FAQ

## What is Yinian?

Yinian (一念, "one thought") is an AI agent that makes anime shorts from a single idea. You tell it your story in a sentence or two, in English or Chinese, and it writes the script, designs the characters, storyboards every shot, generates the images, video and voice, and edits it all into a finished film. You do it all by chatting.

## Who is it for?

Anyone with a story to tell, including people who have never made a film or used an AI tool. Solo creators, story-driven channels and fan-work makers use it to get from idea to episode without a studio's worth of people.

## Do I need any filmmaking or AI experience?

No. You talk to Yinian the way you'd talk to a collaborator, and it handles the production details: script format, shot framing, prompts and editing. If you like fine control, you can still adjust any single shot.

## Does it run on its own, or do I have to approve things?

Both, your choice. By default Yinian shows you its work at the key points (the script, the cast and a sample of the first shots) and asks before any step that costs money. If you set a session budget in Settings, paid steps within that budget are approved automatically, and a spending cap stops everything if costs go beyond what you set.

## What do I get at the end?

A finished mp4 of your short, edited in order with transitions. If you'd rather finish the edit yourself, you can export a package instead, with one folder per shot and a shot list for CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or any other editor.

## How does billing work?

Generation is billed by usage. Each creator can plug in their own video-generation gateway key in Settings, so costs follow the providers you choose — see the in-app pricing details for current rates. Yinian quotes a cost before paid steps, and failed jobs never bill you twice.

## What's the difference between the hosted edition and self-hosting?

- **Hosted** ([yinian.avalidigital.ca](https://yinian.avalidigital.ca)): open an account and start immediately — we run the servers.
- **Self-hosted**: run your own Docker deployment with your own Supabase project for accounts; all data stays on your machine. See [Self-hosting](/self-host).

## Where is my data?

Every account gets an isolated workspace. On the hosted edition workspaces are per-user and isolated from each other; on a self-hosted instance everything stays in your own Docker volume. Your API keys are stored encrypted, and logs never contain them.

## Which languages does the UI support?

English and Chinese, switchable in-app. You can also chat with Yinian in either language.

## I forgot my password

Use "forgot password" on the login page — you'll get a recovery link by email.
